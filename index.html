About Us

Lakbay Batangas is an initiative by the Provincial Tourism and Cultural Affairs Office (PTCAO) of Batangas, Philippines,
designed to promote the province's rich culture, heritage, faith, local craftsmanship, and sustainable tourism through curated itineraries and free shuttle tours.
